Július

The whole month on one sheet, the way it goes up on the wall by the counter. Two rooms and the garden: the cellar for the loud end of it, the front bar and the yard for everything else. Earplugs are free at the cloakroom, no photos on the floor, and the door policy comes down to don't be a problem. Garden goes quiet at midnight so the neighbours stay friends.

Side B1Tue — Sat, 12:00 — 20:00

The shop

The reason the rest of it exists. Techno and house, new and second-hand, Hungarian jazz and funk reissues, local tapes, dub plates from people who were behind the decks here last Saturday, and a 7" box that is uncatalogued on purpose. Every deck in the shop works — point at a sleeve and we'll put it on.

Side B3Daily 12:00 — 24:00

The garden

A walnut tree, eighty chairs that don't match, a wall we project onto, and a pair of speakers kept deliberately quiet. This is where the shop spills out on a Sunday and where everyone ends up at six in the morning. Midnight it empties — the neighbours have been good to us and we'd like to keep it that way.

Find us

Through the gate, across the courtyard, the door with the yellow bell. If it looks closed it is probably open. Tram 4–6 to Wesselényi utca, M2 to Blaha Lujza tér, then four minutes on foot. Step at the gate, none inside — ring the bell and someone will come out with the ramp.
